Duṭṭhadosasikkhāpadaṃ
8. Yo pana bhikkhu bhikkhuṃ duṭṭho appatīto amūlakena pārājikena dhammena anuddhaṃseyya ‘‘appeva naṃ imamhā cāveyya’’nti, tato samayena samanuggāhīyamāno vā vā amūlakañceva taṃ adhikaraṇaṃ hoti, ca dosaṃ patiṭṭhāti, saṅghādiseso.

Dubbacasikkhāpadaṃ
