Methunadhamma
1. Yo pana sikkhāsājīvasamāpanno sikkhaṃ appaccakkhāya dubbalyaṃ anāvikatvā methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭiseveyya, antamaso tiracchānagatāyapi, pārājiko hoti .

Sukkavissaṭṭhisikkhāpadaṃ
1. Sañcetanikā sukkavissaṭṭhi aññatra supinantā saṅghādiseso.
Kāyasaṃsaggasikkhāpadaṃ
2. Yo pana bhikkhu ṇṇo ṇatena cittena mātugāmena saddhiṃ samāpajjeyya hatthaggāhaṃ vā veṇiggāhaṃ vā aññatarassa vā aññatarassa vā aṅgassa parāmasanaṃ, saṅghādiseso.
Duṭṭhullavācāsikkhāpadaṃ
3. Yo pana bhikkhu ṇṇo vipariṇatena cittena mātugāmaṃ duṭṭhullāhi vācāhi obhāseyya yathā taṃ yuvatiṃ methunupasaṃhitāhi, saṅghādiseso.
4. Yo pana bhikkhu otiṇṇo vipariṇatena cittena attakāmapāricariyāya vaṇṇaṃ bhāseyya ‘‘etadaggaṃ bhagini pāricariyānaṃ yā mādisaṃ sīlavantaṃ kalyāṇadhammaṃ etena dhammena paricareyyā’’ti methunupasaṃhitena, .
Sañcarittasikkhāpadaṃ
5. Yo pana bhikkhu sañcarittaṃ samāpajjeyya itthiyā vā purisamatiṃ purisassa vā itthimatiṃ, vā jārattane vā, antamaso taṅkhaṇ, saṅghādiseso.
6. Saññācikāya pana bhikkhunā kuṭiṃ kārayamānena assāmikaṃ attuddesaṃ pamāṇikā kāretabbā, tatridaṃ pamāṇaṃ, dīghaso dvādasa sugatavidatthiyā, , bhikkhū , tehi bhikkhūhi vatthu desetabbaṃ anārambhaṃ saparikkamanaṃ. Sārambhe ce bhikkhu vatthusmiṃ aparikkamane saññācikāya kuṭiṃ kāreyya, vā anabhineyya vatthudesanāya, ṇaṃ vā atikkāmeyya, saṅghādiseso.
Vihārakārasikkhāpadaṃ
7. Mahallakaṃ pana bhikkhunā vihāraṃ kārayamānena sassāmikaṃ attuddesaṃ abhinetabbā vatthudesanāya, bhikkhūhi vatthu desetabbaṃ saparikkamanaṃ. Sārambhe ce bhikkhu vatthusmiṃ aparikkamane mahallakaṃ vihāraṃ kāreyya, bhikkhū vā anabhineyya vatthudesanāya, saṅghādiseso.
Duṭṭhadosasikkhāpadaṃ
8. Yo bhikkhu bhikkhuṃ duṭṭho appatīto amūlakena pārājikena dhammena anuddhaṃseyya ‘‘ nāma naṃ imamhā brahmacariyā cāveyya’’, tato samayena samanuggāhīyamāno vā asamanuggāhīyamāno vā amūlakañceva taṃ adhikaraṇaṃ hoti, bhikkhu ca , saṅghādiseso.
Aññabhāgiyasikkhāpadaṃ
9. Yo pana bhikkhu bhikkhuṃ duṭṭho doso appatīto aññabhāgiyassa adhikaraṇassa kiñcidesaṃ lesamattaṃ pārājikena dhammena anuddhaṃseyya ‘‘appeva nāma naṃ imamhā brahmacariyā cāveyya’’nti, tato aparena samanuggāhīyamāno vā asamanuggāhīyamāno vā aññabhāgiyañceva ṇaṃ hoti kocideso lesamatto upādinno, bhikkhu ca dosaṃ , saṅghādiseso.
10. Yo samaggassa saṅghassa bhedāya parakkameyya, bhedanasaṃvattanikaṃ vā adhikaraṇaṃ samādāya paggayha tiṭṭheyya, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi evamassa vacanīyo ‘‘ samaggassa parakkami, bhedanasaṃvattanikaṃ vā ṇaṃ samādāya paggayha aṭṭhāsi, sametāyasmā saṅghena, samaggo hi saṅgho sammodamāno avivadamāno ekuddeso viharatī’’ti, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi vuccamāno tatheva paggaṇheyya, so bhikkhu yāvatatiyaṃ samanubhāsitabbo tassa paṭinissaggāya, yāvatatiyañce samanubhāsiyamāno taṃ paṭinissajjeyya, iccetaṃ , no ce paṭinissajjeyya, saṅghādiseso.
Bhedānuvattakasikkhāpadaṃ
11. Tasseva kho bhikkhussa bhikkhū honti anuvattakā eko vā vā vā, te evaṃ ‘‘māyasmanto etaṃ bhikkhuṃ kiñci , dhammavādī ceso bhikkhu, vinayavādī bhikkhu, amhākañceso bhikkhu chandañca ruciñca ādāya voharati, jānāti, no bhāsati, amhākampetaṃ khamatī’’ti, te bhikkhū evamassu vacanīyā ‘‘māyasmanto avacuttha, na ceso bhikkhu , na ceso bhikkhu vinayavādī, māyasmantānampi saṅghabhedo ruccittha, saṅghena, hi sammodamāno avivadamāno ekuddeso phāsu viharatī’’ti, evañca te bhikkhū bhikkhūhi vuccamānā tatheva paggaṇheyyuṃ, te bhikkhū bhikkhūhi yāvatatiyaṃ samanubhāsitabbā paṭinissaggāya, yāvatatiyañce samanubhāsiyamānā paṭinissajjeyyuṃ, iccetaṃ kusalaṃ, no ce , saṅghādiseso.
Dubbacasikkhāpadaṃ
12. Bhikkhu paneva dubbacajātiko hoti sikkhāpadesu bhikkhūhi sahadhammikaṃ attānaṃ karoti ‘‘mā maṃ kiñci avacuttha kalyāṇaṃ vā pāpakaṃ vā, ahampāyasmante na kiñci vakkhāmi kalyāṇaṃ vā pāpakaṃ vā, viramathāyasmanto mama ’’ti, so bhikkhu evamassa vacanīyo ‘‘ attānaṃ avacanīyaṃ akāsi, vacanīyamevāyasmā attānaṃ karotu, āyasmāpi bhikkhū sahadhammena, bhikkhūpi āyasmantaṃ vakkhanti , evaṃ saṃvaddhā hi tassa bhagavato yadidaṃ aññamaññavacanena aññamaññavuṭṭhāpanenā’’ti, evañca so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi vuccamāno tatheva ṇheyya, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi yāvatatiyaṃ tassa , yāvatatiyañce samanubhāsiyamāno paṭinissajjeyya, iccetaṃ , no ce paṭinissajjeyya, .
Kuladūsakasikkhāpadaṃ
13. Bhikkhu aññataraṃ gāmaṃ vā nigamaṃ vā upanissāya viharati pāpasamācāro, tassa samācārā dissanti suyyanti ca, kulāni ca tena duṭṭhāni dissanti ceva suyyanti ca, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi evamassa ‘‘ kuladūsako pāpasamācāro, āyasmato kho pāpakā samācārā dissanti ceva suyyanti ca, kulāni cāyasmatā duṭṭhāni dissanti ceva suyyanti ca, pakkamatāyasmā imamhā , alaṃ te idha vāsenā’’ti, evañca so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi te bhikkhū evaṃ vadeyya ‘‘chandagāmino ca bhikkhū, dosagāmino ca bhikkhū, mohagāmino ca bhikkhū, bhayagāmino ca bhikkhū tādisikāya āpattiyā ekaccaṃ pabbājenti, ekaccaṃ na pabbājentī’’ti, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi vacanīyo ‘‘māyasmā evaṃ avaca, na ca bhikkhū chandagāmino, na ca bhikkhū dosagāmino, na ca bhikkhū mohagāmino, na ca bhayagāmino, āyasmā kho kuladūsako , āyasmato kho pāpakā samācārā dissanti ceva suyyanti ca, kulāni dissanti ceva suyyanti ca, pakkamatāyasmā imamhā , alaṃ te vāsenā’’ti, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi vuccamāno tatheva paggaṇheyya, so bhikkhu bhikkhūhi samanubhāsitabbo tassa paṭinissaggāya, yāvatatiyañce samanubhāsiyamāno taṃ paṭinissajjeyya, iccetaṃ , no ce paṭinissajjeyya, saṅghādiseso.
Uddiṭṭhā kho āyasmanto terasa saṅghādisesā dhammā nava paṭhamāpattikā, cattāro yāvatatiyakā. Yesaṃ aññataraṃ vā aññataraṃ vā āpajjitvā yāvatīhaṃ jānaṃ , tāvatīhaṃ tena bhikkhunā akāmā parivatthabbaṃ. Parivutthaparivāsena bhikkhunā uttari paṭipajjitabbaṃ, ciṇṇ bhikkhu yattha siyā vīsatigaṇo , tattha so bhikkhu abbhetabbo. Ekenapi ce ūno vīsatigaṇo bhikkhuṃ abbheyya, so ca bhikkhu anabbhito, te ca gārayhā, ayaṃ tattha sāmīci. Tatthāyasmante pucchāmi, kaccittha parisuddhā, dutiyampi pucchāmi, parisuddhā, tatiyampi , kaccittha parisuddhā, parisuddhetthāyasmanto, tasmā tuṇhī, dhārayāmīti.
Saṅghādiseso niṭṭhito.

Kathinasikkhāpadaṃ
1. Niṭṭhitacīvarasmiṃ bhikkhunā ubbhatasmiṃ kathine dasāhaparamaṃ atirekacīvaraṃ dhāretabbaṃ, taṃ atikkāmayato nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Udositasikkhāpadaṃ
2. Niṭṭhitacīvarasmiṃ ubbhatasmiṃ kathine ekarattampi ce bhikkhu vippavaseyya, aññatra bhikkhusammutiyā nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Akālacīvarasikkhāpadaṃ
3. Niṭṭhitacīvarasmiṃ bhikkhunā ubbhatasmiṃ bhikkhuno paneva akālacīvaraṃ uppajjeyya, ākaṅkhamānena bhikkhunā paṭiggahetabbaṃ, paṭiggahetvā khippameva kāretabbaṃ, no cassa , bhikkhunā taṃ cīvaraṃ nikkhipitabbaṃ ūnassa pāripūriyā paccāsāya. Tato ce uttari nikkhipeyya , nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Purāṇacīvarasikkhāpadaṃ
4. Yo pana bhikkhu aññātikāya ṇacīvaraṃ dhovāpeyya vā rajāpeyya vā ākoṭāpeyya vā, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Cīvarapaṭiggahaṇasikkhāpadaṃ
5. Yo pana aññātikāya bhikkhuniyā hatthato cīvaraṃ paṭiggaṇheyya aññatra pārivattakā, .
Aññātakaviññattisikkhāpadaṃ
6. Yo pana aññātakaṃ vā gahapatāniṃ vā cīvaraṃ viññāpeyya aññatra samayā, pācittiyaṃ. Tatthāyaṃ samayo, acchinnacīvaro vā hoti bhikkhu, naṭṭhacīvaro vā, ayaṃ tattha samayo.
Tatuttarisikkhāpadaṃ
7. Tañce aññātako vā gahapatānī vā bahūhi cīvarehi abhihaṭṭhuṃ pavāreyya, bhikkhunā tato sāditabbaṃ. Tato ce uttari sādiyeyya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Paṭhamaupakkhaṭasikkhāpadaṃ
8. Bhikkhuṃ uddissa aññātakassa gahapatissa vā vā cīvaracetāpannaṃ upakkhaṭaṃ hoti ‘‘iminā cīvaracetāpannena cīvaraṃ itthannāmaṃ bhikkhuṃ cīvarena acchādessāmī’’ti, tatra ce so bhikkhu pubbe cīvare āpajjeyya ‘‘sādhu vata maṃ āyasmā iminā cīvaracetāpannena evarūpaṃ vā evarūpaṃ vā cīvaraṃ cetāpetvā acchādehī’’ti kalyāṇakamyataṃ upādāya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Dutiyaupakkhaṭasikkhāpadaṃ
9. Bhikkhuṃ paneva uddissa ubhinnaṃ aññātakānaṃ gahapatīnaṃ vā gahapatānīnaṃ vā paccekacīvaracetāpannāni honti ‘‘ mayaṃ paccekacīvaracetāpannehi paccekacīvarāni itthannāmaṃ bhikkhuṃ cīvarehi acchādessāmā’’ti, tatra ce so bhikkhu pubbe appavārito cīvare āpajjeyya ‘‘ maṃ āyasmanto imehi paccekacīvaracetāpannehi evarūpaṃ vā evarūpaṃ vā cīvaraṃ cetāpetvā ubhova santā ekenā’’ti kalyāṇ upādāya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ.
Rājasikkhāpadaṃ
10. Bhikkhuṃ paneva uddissa rājā vā rājabhoggo vā brāhmaṇo vā gahapatiko vā dūtena cīvaracetāpannaṃ pahiṇeyya ‘‘iminā cīvaracetāpannena cīvaraṃ itthannāmaṃ bhikkhuṃ cīvarena acchādehī’’ti. So ce dūto taṃ upasaṅkamitvā evaṃ vadeyya ‘‘idaṃ kho, , āyasmantaṃ uddissa cīvaracetāpannaṃ ābhataṃ, ṇhātu āyasmā cīvaracetāpanna’’nti. Tena bhikkhunā so dūto evamassa vacanīyo ‘‘na mayaṃ, āvuso, cīvaracetāpannaṃ paṭiggaṇhāma, cīvarañca kho mayaṃ ṇhāma kappiya’’nti. So ce dūto taṃ bhikkhuṃ evaṃ vadeyya ‘‘ panāyasmato koci veyyāvaccakaro’’ti. , bhikkhave, bhikkhunā veyyāvaccakaro ārāmiko vā upāsako vā ‘‘eso kho, āvuso, bhikkhūnaṃ veyyāvaccakaro’’ti. So ce dūto veyyāvaccakaraṃ saññāpetvā taṃ bhikkhuṃ upasaṅkamitvā evaṃ vadeyya ‘‘yaṃ kho, bhante, āyasmā veyyāvaccakaraṃ niddisi, saññatto so mayā, upasaṅkamatāyasmā kālena, cīvarena taṃ acchādessatī’’ti. Cīvaratthikena, bhikkhave, bhikkhunā veyyāvaccakaro codetabbo sāretabbo ‘‘attho me, āvuso, cīvarenā’’ti, dvattikkhattuṃ taṃ cīvaraṃ abhinipphādeyya, iccetaṃ kusalaṃ, no ce , catukkhattuṃ pañcakkhattuṃ chakkhattuparamaṃ tuṇhībhūtena uddissa ṭhātabbaṃ, catukkhattuṃ pañcakkhattuṃ chakkhattuparamaṃ tuṇhībhūto uddissa tiṭṭhamāno taṃ cīvaraṃ , iccetaṃ kusalaṃ, ce vāyamamāno taṃ cīvaraṃ abhinipphādeyya, nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ. No ce , yatassa cīvaracetāpannaṃ ābhataṃ, sāmaṃ vā gantabbaṃ, dūto vā pāhetabbo ‘‘yaṃ tumhe āyasmanto uddissa cīvaracetāpannaṃ pahiṇittha, na tassa kiñci atthaṃ anubhoti, yuñjantāyasmanto , mā vo ’’ti, ayaṃ tattha sāmīci.
